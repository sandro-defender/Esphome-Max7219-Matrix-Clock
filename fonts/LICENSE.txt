Contains 8-row MAX7219 pixel glyphs for:
- Standard ASCII digits (U+0030..U+0039, 5x7)
- Custom big digits (U+E000..U+E009, 6x8)
- English uppercase letters (U+0041..U+005A)
- English lowercase letters (U+0061..U+007A)
- Georgian Mkhedruli letters (U+10D0..U+10F0)
- Clock and status punctuation (:.-/%!?+ )
